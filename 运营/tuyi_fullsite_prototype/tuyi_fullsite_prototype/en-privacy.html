PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy policy page simulation

This page summarizes the current public policy for layout review. Production must synchronize the full approved policy text and verify actual data flows.

Prototype only. Based on public policy v1.0, effective 2026-10-16. This summary does not replace the full legal text.

Information collected

The published policy lists account information, email verification and reset credentials, device and network-security information, usage data, and credits / billing information.

Data flows

PSD / PSB file bodies and replaced images are processed locally and not uploaded. Layer text, translation-table source and translated text, path strings, and troubleshooting information can leave the device. Smart detection sends source text and current translations to a third-party AI model service.

Retention and user rights

The published policy lists several retention periods, including review snapshots up to 7 days and failed-login / rate-limit records up to 90 days. The current Beta policy says self-service account cancellation is not yet available. Requests can be submitted through the listed email.

This is a layout and content review draft, not legal advice. Confirm the final policy with the operating entity and technical owner.

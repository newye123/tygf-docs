标准流程The workflow

01

导入译文表Import translation tables

整张多语言表，或多张单语言表一次选中自动合并；执行前先查空译文、重复原文、同源不同译。Use one multilingual sheet or several single-language sheets at once. Empty cells, duplicate sources, and conflicting translations are surfaced before execution.

02

选稿与语言Choose drafts and languages

一次最多导入 12 份设计稿；每份稿按自己的表与目标语种执行，单批最多 12 个语种。Import up to 12 drafts at once. Each draft runs against its own table and target languages, with up to 12 languages per batch.

03

批量写入Write in batch

本地助手通过 Photoshop 写入，保留可编辑文字图层；拿不准的位置停下来等你确认。The local assistant writes through Photoshop and keeps text editable. Ambiguous matches stop for your decision.

04

回读与交付Read back and hand off

重开文件回读文字与渲染行数，把待复查、未覆盖、执行失败分开列，最终视觉由你确认。Reopen the file to read back text and rendered line counts. Review, uncovered, and failed items are listed separately. Final visual QA stays with you.

02

03

04

行距与字距按组调Leading and tracking by group

05

换行写回，再回读Line breaks written back, then measured

06

例外集中处理Exceptions in one queue

常见问题FAQ

先把最容易误会的几件事说清楚。A few things worth clearing up first.

它会替我翻译吗？Does it translate for me?

不会。需要你提供已经确认的译文；产品负责把译文写回对应的文字图层。No. You provide approved translations; the product writes them into the matching text layers.

12 份稿是一次跑完吗？Do 12 drafts run in one go?

一次可以准备最多 12 份稿，但每份稿按自己的表和语言分别执行；单批语言上限是 12。Up to 12 drafts can be prepared together, but each runs as its own batch against its own table. Languages per batch are capped at 12.

写完就不用看了吗？Is review unnecessary after writing?

需要。回读核对文字与渲染行数；语义和美观要在 Photoshop 里逐语种看。Yes, review is needed. Read-back covers text and rendered lines; meaning and visual quality are checked per language in Photoshop.

长译文撑版怎么办？What happens when long copy overflows?

可能撑版。放不下的位置进例外清单，不自动缩字号或加行；改版判断留给你。It may overflow. Positions that do not fit go to the exception list; type is not auto-shrunk or re-lined. The layout decision stays with you.

哪些文件不适合？Which files are a poor fit?

文字已栅格化或并层、文字在无法处理的智能对象里、结构复杂且未验证的稿，先别批量跑。Rasterized or flattened text, text in unsupported smart objects, and unverified complex structures should not be batched.

数据安全怎么理解？How should data handling be understood?

文件本体留在本机；图层文字、译文和路径会上传；智能检测会调用第三方模型。以正式政策为准。File bytes stay local; layer text, translations, and paths are uploaded; smart checks call third-party models. The official policy governs.

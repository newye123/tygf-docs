TuYi Workshop · Landing directions 2026-10-28

二十二套方向，同一件产品。Twenty-two directions. One product.

每套都是可以直接打开的完整模拟官网：四个视图（首页、交互示意、适配自查、申请），中英双语，离线单文件。界面里的数据、报告和进度都是示意，用来讨论版式与叙事，不是能力证据。Every entry is a complete, openable mock site: four views (home, interactive demo, fit check, apply), bilingual, offline and self-contained. All numbers, reports and progress inside are schematic material for discussing layout and narrative, not evidence of capability.

共同事实边界：单批最多 12 个语种；12 份稿一次准备但逐稿成批；不自动加行、不静默缩字号；文字回读不等于视觉或语义验收；已在 Photoshop 2021（22.0）验证。Shared boundaries: up to 12 languages per batch; 12 drafts prepared together, batched per draft; no automatic line adding and no silent type shrinking; text read-back is not visual or semantic QA; verified on Photoshop 2021 (22.0).

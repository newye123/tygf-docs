提醒Note

勾满不等于对All ticked is not all right

六项勾满，只能说明你查过，不能说明都对。清单的作用是让漏项没有地方躲，代替不了人最后看一眼。Six ticks mean you checked. They do not prove it is right. A checklist leaves misses nowhere to hide; it cannot replace a final human look.

先跑语言最多的那一页Start with the longest-language page

先把译文最长的几种语言放进去测。它们放得下，其他语言大多也放得下。German, Russian and Portuguese usually run longest. If they fit, most others will.

边界Boundaries

边界Boundaries

写清这些，是为了让你少走一趟弯路。These are here so you waste fewer trips.

  • 语种清单随版本变化，以每次运行时可选的为准。The language list changes between versions; the run tells you what is available.
  • 当地用语是否得体属于人工判断，工具不替你拍板。Whether wording suits a market is a human judgement.
  • 清单不含法律合规项，那部分要另找人。Compliance is not on this list; that needs someone else.
